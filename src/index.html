    Set it up.

    Choose the options, prepare the voter list and set the voting window.

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Check the record.

    Compare the totals and look up your receipt in the shared election record.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE TESTING

A research prototype.

Use Ballot Lab for learning and testing, not real elections. The demo key setup creates all three trustee shares together; it does not establish independent trustees.

A matching record does not prove who voted, that the record is genuine or that the election was fair. Local execution, proof generation and accepted network transactions are separate stages.
