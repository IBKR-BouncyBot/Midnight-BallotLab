02 / CAST A BALLOT

Encrypted vote

Vote / Cast a ballot.

Can trustees read individual votes?

Yes. Any two trustees who cooperate can decrypt individual ballots outside this app, even before the totals are revealed. The app shows combined totals, but the key shares do not prevent this kind of cooperation. In this demo, one browser creates all three trustee shares.

Choose a demo voter, select one option and cast the vote. Each voter can vote once. Your receipt lets you find the encrypted ballot in the election record.
